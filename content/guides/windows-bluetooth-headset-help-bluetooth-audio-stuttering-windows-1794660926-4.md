Your Bluetooth headset stays connected, but the audio cuts in and out while media plays on your Windows 11 PC. This is a common **bluetooth audio stuttering windows** issue, and it usually points to interference or a power-saving setting rather than a dead headset.

## Check for wireless interference

Move the headset and PC away from Wi-Fi routers, USB 3.0 hubs, and other 2.4 GHz devices. Keep the headset within a few feet of the PC. If the stuttering stops, interference was the cause; if it continues, move to the next check.

## Disable Bluetooth power saving

Open Device Manager, expand Bluetooth, right-click your adapter, and choose Properties. On the Power Management tab, clear Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power. Click OK. Then open Settings > System > Power & battery and set Power mode to Balanced or Best performance. If audio is now steady, power saving was the culprit.

## Update or roll back the Bluetooth driver

In Device Manager, right-click the adapter and select Update driver > Search automatically. If Windows reports the best driver is already installed, note the current driver version. If stuttering began after a recent update, choose Properties > Driver > Roll Back Driver. This is your recovery step: if the rollback makes things worse, return to Device Manager and update the driver again to restore the previous state.
