You're playing music or watching a video on your Windows 11 PC with Bluetooth headphones, and the audio keeps cutting in and out even though the headset shows as connected. This is a common **bluetooth headphones stuttering windows 11** problem, and it usually comes down to interference, power settings, or a driver issue.

## Check for Wireless Interference

Bluetooth shares the 2.4 GHz band with Wi-Fi and USB 3.0 devices. Move the headset closer to the PC, and keep it away from USB 3.0 hubs or external drives. If your Wi-Fi uses 2.4 GHz, try switching the router to 5 GHz. Result interpretation: if stuttering stops when you move away from other devices, interference was the cause.

## Disable Bluetooth Power Saving

Open Device Manager, expand Bluetooth, right-click your adapter, and choose Properties. Under the Power Management tab, uncheck "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power." Click OK. Also, go to Settings > System > Power & battery and set Power mode to Best performance. Result interpretation: if the dropout frequency decreases, power saving was interrupting the radio.

## Update or Reinstall the Bluetooth Driver

In Device Manager, right-click your Bluetooth adapter and select Update driver > Search automatically. If that fails, download the latest driver from your PC manufacturer's website. As a last resort, right-click and choose Uninstall device, then restart the PC to reinstall the default driver. Result interpretation: if Device Manager shows no errors and the driver date is recent, the driver is likely not the culprit.
