You're on a Windows 11 PC, your Bluetooth headset shows as connected, but the output device stays silent—here's how to fix that specific **windows 11 bluetooth headphones no sound** issue.

## Check the active output device

Open Settings > System > Sound. Under Output, confirm your Bluetooth headset is selected. If it isn't, choose it from the list. If it is selected but still silent, click the device and check its volume slider—make sure it's not at zero or muted. Also, click the volume icon in the taskbar and verify the correct device is chosen there.

## Verify the headset's own controls

Many headsets have a physical mute switch or volume dial. Check that the headset isn't muted and its volume is turned up. If your headset has a "Stereo" vs. "Hands-Free" mode, try switching to Stereo for playback.

## Run the audio troubleshooter

Go to Settings > System > Sound > Troubleshoot. Select your output device and let Windows attempt a repair. If it finds and fixes an issue, test sound again.

## Verification test

Play a short audio clip (e.g., a system sound or music). While it plays, open Sound settings and watch the volume meter next to your headset. If the meter moves but you hear nothing, the issue is likely the headset's volume or mute. If the meter doesn't move, Windows isn't routing audio to the device—re-select it as the default output.

## Rollback and recovery

If you recently installed a driver or Windows update, roll it back: Device Manager > Bluetooth > your headset > Properties > Driver > Roll Back Driver. If no rollback is available, unpair the headset, restart the PC, and pair it again.
