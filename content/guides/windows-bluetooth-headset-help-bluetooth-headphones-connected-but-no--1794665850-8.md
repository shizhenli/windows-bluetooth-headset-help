You've paired your Bluetooth headset, Windows 11 shows it connected, but the output device stays silent. Let's walk through a focused diagnosis to get sound back.

## Check the Default Output Device

Right-click the speaker icon in the system tray and select Sound settings. Under Output, confirm your Bluetooth headset is selected as the default device. If it's listed but not active, click it and test. If it's not listed at all, proceed to the next check.

## Verify the Headset Is Not in Hands-Free Mode

Windows often creates two entries for a Bluetooth headset: Stereo (high-quality audio) and Hands-Free (for calls). The Hands-Free profile can cause silence in media apps. In Sound settings, under Output, choose the Stereo entry for your headset. Then test playback.

## Run the Audio Troubleshooter

Go to Settings > System > Sound > Troubleshoot. Select your output device and follow the prompts. The troubleshooter can automatically reset the audio service and re-enable disabled devices. If it finds and fixes an issue, restart your PC and test again.

## Disable Audio Enhancements

In Sound settings, click your headset, then Advanced. Uncheck Enable audio enhancements. Some drivers conflict with enhancements, causing silence. Click OK and test.

## Verification Test

Play a short audio clip or use the Test button in Sound settings. You should hear sound clearly. If still silent, try another app (e.g., a video) to rule out app-specific issues.

## Rollback/Recovery Note

If changes make things worse, use System Restore to revert to a previous point. Or, in Device Manager, right-click your Bluetooth adapter and select Uninstall device, then restart to reinstall the driver.
