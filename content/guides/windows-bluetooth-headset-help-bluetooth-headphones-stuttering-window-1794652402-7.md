You're playing music or a video on your Windows 11 PC, and your Bluetooth headset keeps cutting in and out even though it stays connected. This is a common **bluetooth headphones stuttering windows 11** issue, and it usually comes down to interference, power management, or a driver conflict.

## Check for Wireless Interference

First, move your headset closer to the PC and away from other wireless devices (Wi‑Fi routers, USB 3.0 hubs, microwaves). If the stuttering stops, interference was the cause. Also, open Device Manager, expand Bluetooth, right‑click your adapter, and select Properties > Advanced. If you see a Bluetooth radio mode setting, try switching between 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz if available. Result: if the dropouts disappear after moving, keep the headset within a clear line of sight.

## Disable Power Saving on the Bluetooth Adapter

In Device Manager, right‑click your Bluetooth adapter and choose Properties > Power Management. Uncheck "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power." Click OK. Then go to Settings > System > Power & battery > Power mode and set it to Best performance. Result: this prevents Windows from throttling the adapter during playback.

## Update or Roll Back the Bluetooth Driver

In Device Manager, right‑click your Bluetooth adapter and select Update driver > Search automatically. If that doesn't help, select Properties > Driver > Roll Back Driver (if available). Result: a newer driver may fix stuttering; a rollback restores a previous working version if an update caused the issue.

## Verification Test

Play a local audio file for two minutes. If the sound stays steady without cutting out, the fix worked. If stuttering returns, repeat the interference check with only the headset connected.

## Rollback Note

If disabling power saving or updating the driver makes things worse, return to Device Manager and re‑enable the power saving option or use Roll Back Driver to restore the previous state.
