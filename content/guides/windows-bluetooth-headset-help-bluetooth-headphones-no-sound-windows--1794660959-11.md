Your Windows 11 PC shows the Bluetooth headset connected, but the output device stays silent.  

## Check the active output device
Open Settings > System > Sound. Under Output, confirm your headset is selected. If it is not, click it. If it is selected but still silent, click the arrow next to it and ensure the correct profile (Stereo) is chosen, not Hands-Free. Result: the headset should appear as the default output with a moving volume bar when audio plays.

## Run the audio troubleshooter
Go to Settings > System > Sound > Troubleshoot common sound problems. Select your headset when prompted. Result: the troubleshooter may restart the audio service or report a specific driver issue. If it finds nothing, continue.

## Re-pair the headset
Remove the device: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > your headset > Remove device. Put the headset in pairing mode, then add it again. Result: a fresh pairing often clears a stuck profile. This is a common fix for **bluetooth headphones no sound windows 11** when the connection looks fine but audio is routed incorrectly.

## Verify with a test sound
After re-pairing, play a short system sound (Settings > System > Sound > More sound settings > Play test sound). Result: you should hear the chime through the headset. If not, check the headset's own volume and mute button.

## Rollback note
If audio worked before a recent driver or Windows update, open Device Manager > Sound, video and game controllers > your Bluetooth audio device > Properties > Driver > Roll Back Driver. Restart and retest.
