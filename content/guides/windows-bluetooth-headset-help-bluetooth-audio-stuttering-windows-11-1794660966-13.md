You're playing music or a video on your Windows 11 PC, and the Bluetooth headset keeps dropping audio for a second or two even though it never disconnects. This is a common **bluetooth audio stuttering windows 11** issue, and it usually comes down to power management or interference.

## Check the obvious first

Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Devices and click your headset. Make sure it's set as the default output device. Then right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar, choose Sounds > Playback, and confirm the headset is the active device. If it is, move on.

## Disable Bluetooth power saving

Open Device Manager, expand Bluetooth, right-click your adapter, and choose Properties. Under the Power Management tab, uncheck "Allow the computer to turn off this device to save power." Click OK. Then do the same for the headset under Sound, video and game controllers if it appears there. This stops Windows from briefly throttling the radio during playback.

## Reduce interference and update the driver

Keep the headset within a few feet of the PC and away from USB 3.0 ports and Wi-Fi routers. In Device Manager, right-click the Bluetooth adapter and select Update driver > Search automatically. If Windows finds nothing, visit your PC maker's support page for the latest Bluetooth driver.

## Verify the fix

Play a local audio file for two minutes without touching the mouse. If the sound stays steady, the stutter is resolved. If it returns, roll back the driver via Device Manager > Properties > Driver > Roll Back Driver, then test again.
