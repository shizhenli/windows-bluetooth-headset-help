symptoms:
- title: Connected but no sound
  text: Windows shows the headset as connected, but audio still comes from the laptop speakers.
- title: Microphone is not detected
  text: Playback works, but the headset microphone is missing from recording devices or apps.
- title: Sound drops after reconnect
  text: The headset works once, then disappears or switches to hands-free mode after sleep.
checks:
- title: Set the default output
  text: Open Sound settings and select the headset's stereo output, not a hands-free/communications profile. Check per-app volume and mute.
- title: Test the microphone separately
  text: Choose the headset microphone as input and record a short voice memo. Watch the input meter while speaking.
- title: Remove stale pairings
  text: Unpair old duplicates, then pair once and test. A headset can appear twice with different profiles.
- title: Test reconnect
  text: Disconnect, reconnect, and play the same sample. Check whether the default device changed after sleep.

excluded:

faqs:
- q: Why are there two headset entries?
  a: Bluetooth audio often exposes a stereo output and a hands-free communications profile. Choose the correct one for playback and microphone.
- q: Should I disable hands-free mode?
  a: Not blindly. Use it when the microphone is required; test both profiles and document which one matches the task.
- q: Can you fix a broken headset?
  a: No. We isolate whether Windows, the pairing, or the hardware is failing; physical repair is outside scope.
